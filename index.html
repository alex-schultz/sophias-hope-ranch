About Sophia's Hope Ranch

Sophia’s Hope Ranch is a faith-centered nonprofit therapeutic ranch dedicated to restoring hope, healing, and emotional wellness for individuals and families impacted by trauma, mental health challenges, relational disruption, grief, and adverse life experiences.

Our Mission

We help children, adults, and families heal from trauma. Through faith-centered counseling, time with horses, and a caring community, we walk with people from hurt to hope.

Our Services

We are honored to serve veterans, first responders, adoptive and foster families, and others who are seeking healing from trauma and attachment-related challenges. From our home in Boerne, we serve families across San Antonio and the Texas Hill Country.

Trauma Recovery

Trust-Based Relational Intervention (TBRI) for vulnerable children who have experienced adversity, toxic stress, or trauma. Employs evidence-based strategies to build trust and foster healthy relationships.

Individual Therapy

Tailored sessions for personal healing and growth; available for children, adults, and siblings.

Family Therapy

Strengthening family bonds and addressing collective challenges through personalized therapy sessions.

Couples Therapy

Reviving intimacy, building teamwork, and realizing your family's vision. Strong focus on communication and recognizing and understanding each other's perspective.

Equine Therapy

Leverage unique sensitivity of horses to facilitate emotional, mental, and physical well-being. Strengthens trust and emotional resilience via human-horse interactions.

Play Therapy

Use children's natural medium of expression to help them communicate and resolve emotional or behavioral issues. Facilitates emotional expression and healing through guided play activities.

Expressive Arts Therapy

Integrates multiple artistic modalities, including visual arts, music, dance, drama, poetry, and storytelling to promote growth, development, and healing.

Workshop: Breaking Generational Curses

Addressing and overcoming patterns crossed through generations. We evaluate environmental and spiritual influences to break free of generational strongholds.

Workshop: Parenting

Learn and develop new strategies for parenting. Connecting, empowering, and correcting are the main pillars for this workshop.

Treatment Programs

Families are our clients; not just an individual. Our structured programs are designed for lasting transformation, so individualized treatment plans are developed for all members of the family.

Extensive Outpatient Program

For children ages 2 and up and adults, offering comprehensive support to address attachment and trauma.

  • Individual and family therapy sessions
  • Parenting skills training
  • Trauma-informed care
  • Spiritual guidance and support

Extended Stay Program

For individuals (ages 5+) or families. Providing immersive, 24/7 care in a supportive, therapeutic environment. Focuses on deep healing and personal growth, helping to navigate challenges while engaging in a structured, faith-centered recovery process.

  • Daily therapy sessions
  • Group therapy programs
  • Crisis intervention support
  • Equine Therapy

Board of Directors

Meet the people guiding our mission of healing and hope.

Theresa Schultz, LCSW

President and Founder

Theresa is a Christ-follower, mother, and clinical therapist. With five grown children (two adopted, three biological) and five amazing grandchildren, she has always been drawn to caring for children and families in need.

"For over 30 years, I've served in many roles - foster mom, CASA volunteer, board member, and therapist, each one deepening my passion for helping children heal. As a trauma and attachment specialist, and as a mom to a daughter who experienced attachment disruption, I’ve witnessed the heartbreaking struggle to build safe, connected relationships. Sophia’s Hope Ranch was born from both the pain and the hope of that journey, a reflection of my lifelong calling to help families heal."

Amber Griffin-Colvin

Vice President

Amber is a devoted wife, mother, and proud grandmother whose deep love for people shines through in everything she does. As a successful and hard-working realtor, she is known for serving her clients with integrity, compassion, and excellence. Beyond her professional life, Amber is a generous and active member of the community with a heart for philanthropy.

Her passion for children and her faith in Jesus makes her a natural fit for the mission of Sophia’s Hope Ranch. Amber brings a spirit of encouragement, service, and wisdom to the board, helping to ensure the vision of creating a healing and hope-filled environment for families becomes a reality.

Chet Marko

Treasurer

Chet is a 35-year resident of Boerne, husband to an awesome wife named Paige, father to seven kids, and grandfather to 17 grandkids. Found the Lord at Texas Tech through College Young life and served in leadership with College Young life. After graduating college, he served as a youth director and later committed several years to Missions in Japan and Mexico. After returning, he settled into Health Insurance and soon launched his own Health Insurance Brokerage called Smart Choice Benefits. He and Paige have had an interesting path as lay ministers. We have worked with youth ministry, singles ministry, divorce ministry, sexual abuse recovery, and helping those who struggle with mental health. We believe God loves us where we are and has a beautiful plan to lead us to understand the power of his love, and healing.

Alex Schultz

Secretary

Alex is a software engineer and real estate investor who serves as Secretary of the board. As the ranch's technology lead, Alex built and maintains the very website you're reading now, working behind the scenes to make sure the ranch's story of healing and hope reaches the families who need it most.

Joan Burton

Director, Public Relations

Joan has spent much of her life immersed in the worlds of equestrian sports, yachting, and luxury publishing. A lifelong entrepreneur, her ventures have also included life as a restaurateur, custom furniture designer, and commercial interior design.

Her equestrian journey began at just two years old. She spent decades competing in the hunter/jumper world before transitioning to polo 16 years ago. It was through her work with horses and children with special needs that she discovered a deeper calling—one that extended far beyond the polo field.

What began as a passion for horses evolved into a heartfelt commitment to supporting families in need and reaching out to underserved members of the community. Through the unique connection between horses and people, Joan found a meaningful way to help others find confidence, connection, and healing.

Her philosophy is simple, heartfelt, and always close to her heart:

May the Horse Be with You.

Diana Harris

Director, Equine Programs

Diana holds an MBA in Healthcare management and a BS in Healthcare Administration. She currently oversees administration for Kresta Roofing, Kresta Investment properties, and Kresta Investments, and has consulted in healthcare using Baldrige principles. With over 20 years of equine experience in training, barrel racing, team roping, and horse care, Diana previously owned and managed a horse export facility, coordinating international sales to Europe, Qaisar, Dubai, and Kuwait. She brings a rare combination of business leadership, healthcare expertise, and hands-on equine knowledge to the Board, advancing the organization's mission of healing and growth through equine-assisted services.
